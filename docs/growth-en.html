LINGLU / FAMILY CONVERSATIONS

Growth Notes · Family Conversation

Turn a saved family conversation into a readable report with verifiable excerpts.

Prepare a recordingConfigure a modelRead and verifySave and exportTroubleshoot

Screenshots show the current software interface with controlled example recordings and report content, not observations about a real family. Use your own recording and available model. The English workbench screenshot displays a previously saved Chinese report.

1. Save the recording and transcript

If you do not have a saved conversation, follow the speech-to-text tutorial to transcribe and save one. You can also import an existing recording and complete file transcription first.

Listen to the recording and correct the transcript and speaker labels. Quotations, times and labels in the report come from this saved transcript. Misheard words affect the observations, and the model cannot authenticate the person speaking.

Open the meeting area, expand LingLu and click Growth Notes. Choose the saved conversation. Opening the workbench or loading an existing report does not make a model request.

2. Choose an available report model

Expand Report model settings. If shared minutes settings are already configured, click Import shared minutes settings to copy the provider, model ID, address and key. Changes here apply to the current report and leave shared settings intact.

You can also enter settings directly. Start your local text model service before using it. Cloud services require an available model ID and API key. Growth Notes processes transcript text and does not download or start the text model automatically.

Generate sends the full transcript in batches to the chosen service. Long conversations may need several requests. To keep processing on your machine, verify that the address points to a local service.

Available providers include Ollama, OpenAI-compatible services, DeepSeek, Kimi, MiniMax, Volcengine, GLM, OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini, plus vLLM, MLX, SGLang and LlamaEdge. Model availability depends on your service and account.

3. Read the report and check the original words

Each chapter starts with transcript observations and a short account of the conversation. Seeds identify ideas worth nurturing. A possible AHA moment appears when supported by the transcript. Memorable words preserve exact quotations.

Philosophical, historical, scientific, psychological and ethical lenses offer questions for discussion. Unsupported lenses remain empty. The report does not automatically search for external studies or historical sources; interpret these lenses in context.

Click a citation number to jump to the corresponding excerpt in the full transcript. Check the speaker and timestamp, then listen to the recording. Citation validation confirms that a quote exists in the supplied text, not that the model’s interpretation is correct.

4. Save, stop and share

Completed reports and their transcript snapshots are saved with the recording. Reopen the workbench and select the same recording to load the report without another model request.

Use Stop while generating. Closing a window that started a job also requests cancellation. Closing a view connected to another window’s existing job does not automatically stop that job. Stop becomes unavailable once saving begins.

Click Export report, choose a normal working folder and save an HTML file. The logo, styles and source transcript are included, so it opens offline as a single file. Browser printing can save it as PDF.

If the transcript or speaker labels change later, the saved report keeps the snapshot used for generation. The workbench marks this change. Review the updated transcript before generating again; previous reports are retained separately.

If something goes wrong

  • No recordings available? Complete and save a transcript, then refresh the recording list.
  • Generate disabled? Choose a recording and fill in the service address and model ID.
  • Quotes do not match or the format is invalid? The new result is not published. Your previous report remains available; retry or try another model.
  • The transcript changed during generation? Reload and review the current recording before trying again.
  • Job status uncertain? Connect to the active job before starting another request.
  • A segment or conversation exceeds the processing limit? Split and save it in manageable parts. The software reports the limit instead of silently truncating the transcript.
