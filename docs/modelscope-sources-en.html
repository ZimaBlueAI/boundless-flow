Prepare models from ModelScope

Choose a task, model and source in Setup. The repository is filled in for you. Download to your chosen disk, then register the local model.

Five steps

  1. Open Setup → Models & parameters → Download models & components.
  2. Choose ModelScope, then a task and model shortcut. All 13 shortcuts have a verified source mapping.
  3. Choose your model directory, then Inspect download list. Inspection retrieves metadata without downloading weights.
  4. Review sizes and selected files, then explicitly choose Download selected. Defaults select only SenseVoice model.int8.onnx + tokens.txt, Whisper ggml-base.bin, or HY-MT2 Q4_K_M.
  5. After downloading, add the model to the local library, inspect it, prepare native files where requested, and try a short recording. A completed download does not prove inference readiness.

A few useful distinctions

SenseVoice and Whisper use explicitly labeled community mirrors. Published SHA-256 hashes of recommended model weights match upstream; this does not endorse the uploader. SenseVoice requires ONNX rather than raw model.pt. Whisper requires whisper.cpp GGML files.

IndexTTS and VoxCPM2 may require native-file preparation. Original emotion2vec model.pt requires offline export to a labeled classifier.onnx. Full MOSS-TTS generation remains unavailable.

Changing model or source resets the repository revision instead of carrying over a previous commit. Collapsing the panel or navigating Setup retains custom settings and jobs without automatically restarting downloads. master is a mutable branch; inspect the list before each download.

Read each model’s own license and usage conditions. Catalog inclusion, hash checks and a successful download do not establish commercial permission.
