Proofreading & summaries

Retain the recognition transcript and treat AI output as suggestions to review.

Configure and run

  1. Configure an available LLM service, endpoint, model and authentication in API or advanced settings. A translation model may not be appropriate for long summaries.
  2. Use the appropriate proofreading or summary action in Raw Text or the meeting area, wait for completion and inspect any error.
  3. Compare names, numbers, negation and quotations with the transcript before saving or exporting.

Three different results

Feature / messageGuidance
ProofreadingCorrect wording and recognition mistakes; compare each passage with its source.
SummaryCondense the key information; it does not retain every original sentence.
Minutes / LingLu reportsOrganize sections, decisions and actions; use their dedicated generation, saving and export workflows.

Retain existing content on failure

For timeouts, authentication errors or empty results, keep the transcript and existing drafts and retry after correcting configuration. Verify the selected session. After switching sessions, do not overwrite the new session with a previous draft.
