本政策说明 Boundless Flow 如何处理录音、转写、语音输入、会议与灵录、语音生成、声音克隆、同传、说话人分析和情绪辅助分析中的信息，以及本政策页面的访问。第三方模型、API、托管平台和 Microsoft Store 的独立处理，由相应提供方的政策规定。本政策不表示这些第三方会遵循与本地处理相同的保存或训练规则。

本文描述当前桌面版功能，不将尚未提供的功能或研发计划视为正在进行的数据处理。

三、可选模型服务、API 与下载

服务地址可能是本机、局域网中的另一台设备，也可能是公网或境外服务。启用远程识别、翻译、文本校正、摘要、语音生成或克隆后，软件会将该任务所需内容及调用参数发送到配置的端点，并按接口要求使用你的凭证。

服务提供方通常能够看到请求的网络来源地址，并可能保存请求或错误记录。其处理地区、留存、训练用途、费用和删除方式由该服务决定。选择境外端点可能涉及跨境传输；请在发送前核对接收方、地址、服务条款及隐私政策。公网端点宜使用 HTTPS。

四、录音、语音输入与声音分析

麦克风用于你启动的录音任务，并受 Windows 设备权限控制。语音输入可能将识别或整理后的文字送入你正在使用的其他应用；启用时请确认输入目标，尤其是含有私密信息的文本。

关闭窗口或面板不一定停止录音、同传或已提示在后台进行的整理任务。请使用相应停止或取消操作，并留意任务状态。关闭系统麦克风权限可阻止后续麦克风采集，但不会删除已经保存的文件。

请在取得相应权利与有效许可后处理他人的声音和会议资料，并向相关人员说明使用方式。声纹候选、情绪结果和 AI 摘要都可能出错，不应独立用于认定身份、作出影响个人权益的决定或代替专业判断。

五、合成声音的来源标记

已接入该流程的部分合成、设计或克隆输出会自动添加低感知来源水印。当前流程封装生成编号，并在本机保存对应编号、时间和音频摘要等来源记录；平台方可以使用其离线工具和持有的私钥验证相应载荷。客户端不因此向平台请求或下载私钥，也不因这一流程自动上传生成记录。

当前来源标记流程不自动采集公网 IP、MAC 地址或硬件序列号。实时同传输出不加入这一水印流程。原始参考录音和模型原文件不因加水印被改写。

生成编号可以关联相应输出和本地记录，不应视为绝对匿名信息。水印不证明实际操作者或说话人的身份，也不证明内容真实；未检出水印不证明音频来自真人。转码、剪辑等处理可能破坏检出。

六、保存、导出和清理

配置、任务记录、录音、生成结果和缓存按功能保存在应用用户数据目录或你选择的项目、模型、输出与备份目录。部分任务会保留原始音频、派生音频或来源记录，以便回听、恢复或复用。

这些本地内容通常保留到你主动删除、相关功能执行清理或覆盖相应文件时。并非全部记录都具有固定自动到期时间；不要假定清空界面、删除一条列表记录或卸载应用会同时清除所有原文件、导出、备份及缓存。清理前请先停止相关任务，并核对实际保存位置。

你可以通过软件现有功能和操作系统管理本地文件，关闭相应功能或权限、修改服务配置，并在不再需要时移除凭证。导出与分享会产生独立副本；发送后，接收方可继续持有这些副本。第三方服务留存的内容需向相应服务方申请处理。

我们无法远程读取或删除仅保存在你设备上的资料。若你主动向我们提交了故障材料或隐私请求，我们会为处理该事项而使用，按处理需要和适用要求留存，并处理相应的访问、更正或删除请求；请勿提交不必要的整段录音、未脱敏凭证或私钥。

English privacy policy

Effective and updated: 22 October 2026 · Boundless Flow desktop v0.5.0

Boundless Flow is operated by 齐码蓝智能科技（大理市）有限责任公司, under the ZimaBlueAI brand. Contact bf-support@zimablueai.com for privacy requests, support and AI-content reports.

Local processing and optional services

Local-model inference runs on your device. If you enable a configured model service or API, the audio, text, reference voice, prompts and parameters required for that task are sent to that endpoint, which may be local, on your network, or operated by a third party abroad. Its retention, training, fees and privacy terms apply separately. The provider can normally observe your request's network source address. Review the endpoint and provider before sending content; use HTTPS for public endpoints. Models and components are prepared separately; download providers process their own network requests.

Information used

Depending on the features you use, the application processes recordings and imported audio; transcripts, corrections, translations and project documents; reference voices, speaker features, labels and emotion estimates; settings, model paths, endpoints and API credentials; and task states, timestamps, file paths, errors and local records. These support the selected transcription, input, meeting, report, translation, synthesis and analysis tasks. Local speech inference itself does not require uploading that content to us. This policy does not authorize us to use your content for model training; review third-party providers' terms separately.

Recording, input and analysis

Recording tasks you start use the microphone subject to Windows permissions. Speech input may deliver text to another application; check the target before using it. Closing a panel or window may leave a recording, interpretation or announced background task running; use its Stop or Cancel controls. Obtain the appropriate rights and permissions before using other people's recordings or voices. Speaker and emotion results and AI summaries may be wrong; they do not authenticate identity or provide medical or psychological diagnoses.

Synthesis provenance

Some integrated synthesis, voice-design and cloning outputs automatically include a low-perceptibility provenance watermark. The current integration seals a generation identifier and retains related identifiers, timestamps and audio hashes locally. Platform-held offline tools and private keys can verify the corresponding payload. This workflow does not automatically upload the ledger or collect a public IP address, MAC address or hardware serial number; client software does not download platform private keys. Live speech-translation output is excluded from this watermark workflow. Original reference audio and model files are not modified by it. Identifiers can link records and outputs and are not necessarily anonymous. Detection does not authenticate a speaker or operator or establish the truth of the content. Absence is not proof of human speech; transcoding and editing may prevent detection.

Storage, retention and your controls

Data is stored, as appropriate, in application user-data folders and project, model, output or backup locations you choose. Some tasks retain source audio, derivatives or provenance records. Local data normally remains until you delete it or a relevant feature clears or replaces it; not all records have automatic expiry. Clearing a view, removing a list entry or uninstalling is not a guarantee that every recording, export, backup or cache is erased. Stop active tasks and check storage locations before cleanup. Use available application controls and operating-system tools to manage files, disable features or permissions, change services and remove credentials. Exports and recipients can retain separate copies. Contact third-party services about data they retain.

We cannot remotely access or erase data held only on your device. If you voluntarily send support material or a privacy request, we use it to handle that matter, retain it as needed for that purpose and applicable requirements, and handle appropriate access, correction and deletion requests. Send only necessary information, not unredacted credentials, private keys or unnecessary recordings.

Security, minors and changes

Protect device access, filesystem permissions, backup locations and service endpoints. Encrypted watermark payloads do not mean every recording, setting or report is encrypted. Minors should use recording, cloning, speaker analysis and third-party services with guardian guidance and the necessary permissions. We update this policy when processing changes and provide any additional notice or authorization required before relevant feature use. This policy does not replace permissions required for individual recordings, cloning or data transfers.
